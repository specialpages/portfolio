Good to know

Questions before we begin?

If you have something specific in mind, send over your brief and I'll help you figure out the next step.

Can you customize a website for my business?

Yes. The layout, content, colours and features can be adapted to your business and audience. The final scope is agreed before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile?

The designs are planned to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens. The final result should be reviewed on the devices relevant to your audience.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the number of pages, features, content and feedback rounds. I'll give you an estimate after understanding the brief.

What should I send to get started?

Share your website goal, business or project name, preferred style, example websites you like, content you already have, and any required features.
